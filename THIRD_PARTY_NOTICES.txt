Canon BLE remote references
==========================

M5Stack-AtomS3-BLE-Remote-for-Canon (workflow reference)
Author: Taraskad3o (copyright holder: Taraska)
https://github.com/Taraskad3o/M5Stack-AtomS3-BLE-Remote-for-Canon
https://github.com/Taraskad3o/M5Stack-AtomS3-BLE-Remote-for-Canon/tree/62e8d75ccd262f42744e6b7d4708bb6e89cdc9c4

The pairing and photo/autofocus/movie workflow was used as a reference.
The AI Passport interface and three-button navigation are redesigned for its
portrait screen, and the BLE transport is implemented using ESP-IDF NimBLE.
